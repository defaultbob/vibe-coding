Buttons

28px high utility buttons with 4px radius and 12px medium type. Three variants only — primary, default, and ghost; destructive actions reuse the default button (no dedicated danger fill).

Primary & Defaults
Primary actions use Vault Blue. Secondary operations use bordered default buttons.
Interactive States
Disabled actions use low contrast silver backdrops. Hover states are dense and structured.
Split Buttons
Dual-zone action: the left fires the primary action, the right opens a caret menu. Comes in primary (blue) and secondary (gray) themes — used for the global header "Create" control.
Icon Buttons
Ghost square (4px radius) or round icon-only controls — 28px container / 18px glyph by default. The help variant is a question-circle chip that shows a tooltip on activate.
